HpiSettingsView lets the user enter the coil frequencies (Hz), tweak the goodness-of-fit threshold below which a coil is rejected, toggle continuous HPI tracking and trigger single-shot HPI fits. Live coil GoF values and resulting head-movement vectors come back through Qt signals and are mirrored in the panel's status labels.
