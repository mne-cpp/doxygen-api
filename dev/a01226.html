Detailed Description

BrainFlow's rows as an acquisition stream, and BrainFlow's multicast streamer.

A BrainFlow board delivers samples of num_rows doubles: EEG and other channels in the board's units (microvolts for ExG), a package counter, the timestamp in seconds since 1970 and a marker. Its streamer sends them to a UDP address as datagrams of whole samples, double after double in the sender's byte order.
