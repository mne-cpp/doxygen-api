Detailed Description

Thread placement and priority for acquisition threads (control path).

A thread that the scheduler moves to another core loses its caches, and on a multi-socket machine it may end up reading the ring across the socket link. Pinning the producer and its readers to cores of the ring's NUMA node (AcqStreamOptions::numaNode) keeps the data local; a raised priority keeps other processes from delaying them.
