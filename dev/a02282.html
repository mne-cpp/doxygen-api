MNELIB::MNECTFCompDataSet aggregates the MNELIB::MNECTFCompData entries shipped with a CTF dataset and produces, given a target compensation grade, the channel-aligned compensator that must be applied to raw or forward data. Mirrors make_compensator in the MNE-Python and MNE-C codebases.
