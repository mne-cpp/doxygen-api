Sub-space scanning algorithms (RAP-MUSIC, TRAP-MUSIC, PWL-RAP-MUSIC) return groups of N coupled dipoles together with their mutual correlation. INVLIB::InvSourceCoupling stores the grid indices, the per-source dipole moments and the N×N correlation matrix for one such tuple, plus the time window over which the coupling was evaluated. An INVLIB::InvSourceEstimate may carry many of these (one per iteration of the scan) so multi-dipole solutions remain interpretable downstream.
