synthetic:// : a seeded auditory-oddball EEG/MEG session.

Every sample is a pure function of the seed, the sample index and the channel, so a recording does not depend on block size, pacing or restarts. Signal per channel c (1 model unit = 1 uV on EEG, 20 fT on MEG): 

x_c(s) = noise * 10 * N(0, 1) + 8 * sin(2 pi alpha t + d(t) + phi_c) + evoked * w_c * sum_k erp_k(s)

with t = s / sfreq and d(t) a smooth phase drift (cosine-interpolated random values every 0.5 s, within +-1.5 pi), so the rhythm is not phase-locked to the stimuli, as in real recordings. w_c = cos(pi c / n) (a dipolar topography over the n channels of one kind). Trial k starts at (k + 1 + 0.1 u_k) * interval seconds; it is a target with probability targets, a standard otherwise. Standards evoke an N100 (-5 at 100 ms, sigma 25 ms) and a P200 (+4 at 200 ms, sigma 40 ms); targets add a P300 (+8 at 350 ms, sigma 80 ms). The stim channel holds the trial code (1 standard, 2 target) for 5 ms.

Further modalities are separate streams at their own rates, like the separate amplifiers of a multimodal session, all on one clock domain so their samples map onto each other exactly (rates must then be whole Hz; the domain tick is their least common multiple). Each stream carries the trial events at the first of its samples at or after the onset. Per stimulus (flash and tone of a visual-auditory oddball):

  • fnirs: source-detector pairs with HbO and HbR (M): canonical haemodynamic response (peak 5 s, undershoot 15 s, HbR -0.3 x HbO), Mayer waves (0.1 Hz), respiration (0.25 Hz), cardiac pulsation (1 Hz).
  • ecog (V): the ERP at 4x the EEG amplitude plus a high-gamma burst (80-140 Hz, 150 ms after onset) on a focal group of contacts.
  • erg (V): the flash ERG: a-wave (-80 uV at 15 ms), b-wave (+160 uV at 40 ms), oscillatory potentials (120 Hz around 30 ms).
