mne::MNELayout is the C++ counterpart of MNE-C's mneLayout (mne_layout.c) and reads the same files as mne.channels.read_layout. The first line holds the extent of the drawing area; every further line describes one viewport: number, lower-left corner, width, height (negative to invert the trace) and the channel names drawn in it, separated by colons.
