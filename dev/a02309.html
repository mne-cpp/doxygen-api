MNELIB::MNEEventList is the C++ counterpart of MNE-C's mneEventList (mne_events.c). Unlike the plain sample/before/after matrix of FIFFLIB::FiffEvents it keeps a comment per event, reads the comments stored in -eve.fif files and in MNE-C text event files, and removes the skipped samples at the start of a recording from the sample numbers.
