Neuromag/MEGIN files name the same channel "MEG 0113" or "MEG0113", depending on the software that wrote them. Names are read as stored; files of either convention are combined by matching names as MNE-C does: two names denote the same channel if they are equal once spaces are dropped and letter case is ignored.
