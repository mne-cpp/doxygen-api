ElectrodeObject draws an IEEGLIB::IeegImplantation. It provides two instanced streams for the renderer: one sphere per contact, and the lead bodies (one cylinder along each depth lead, thin ties between neighbouring strip and grid contacts). Depth leads and subdural arrays thus differ by shape, while colour tells one lead from another.

The implantation owns the contacts and leads; this object only adds display state: per-contact colours (e.g. from setContactValues()), the selected and the hovered contact.

Positions are in metres, in the frame of the brain surfaces (FreeSurfer surface RAS).
