Detailed Description

The averages of the selected channels of the shown modalities drawn on top of each other, with the stimulus line, the baseline and the time axis, as a SceneFigure.

Each channel is scaled by its entry in the scale map; each average is coloured by its comment and drawn only if its comment is active. Modalities are "MAG", "GRAD", "EEG", "EOG" and "MISC"; channels of modalities missing from the modality map are hidden. The figure is not zoomable: it lays the ButterflyPlot out to the view size. picture() renders it at any size, e.g. to export it with write_svg() or to_raster().
