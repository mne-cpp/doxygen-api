FIFFLIB::FiffInfo is the C++ counterpart of MNE-Python's mne.Info dictionary. It collects everything stored under FIFFB_MEAS / FIFFB_MEAS_INFO of a FIFF file: per-channel descriptors (FIFFLIB::FiffChInfo via FIFFLIB::FiffInfoBase), sampling frequency, lowpass / highpass filter cutoffs, line frequency, measurement date, subject info, experimenter, projector list (FIFFLIB::FiffProj), CTF software compensation list (FIFFLIB::FiffCtfComp), HPI fit results, the device→head and CTF compensation transforms, the FIFFB_ISOTRAK digitization (FIFFLIB::FiffDigPoint), and the per-channel calibration vectors.

Every higher-level container in FIFFLIB (FIFFLIB::FiffRawData, FIFFLIB::FiffEvoked, FIFFLIB::FiffCov, FIFFLIB::FiffEvokedSet) carries a FIFFLIB::FiffInfo so that downstream consumers (filtering, source localization, plotting, BIDS export) can interpret the data without re-reading the file.
