The data server, on the acquisition computer, sends every client a message per FIFF tag over a UNIX datagram socket. The data follow in a second datagram, lie in a block of the server's shared memory, or are in the file being written (big-endian). The collector, a TCP service, starts and stops measurements and sets the buffer length.
