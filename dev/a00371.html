RhiFrameRenderer executes a scene::GpuFrame with QRhi.

The QRhi backend of the GPU frame description of mne_scene: it keeps one QRhi buffer, texture and pipeline per resource handle, uploads what changed, packs the uniform blocks of all draws into one dynamic uniform buffer and records the passes. Shaders are the precompiled .qsb files of qt_add_shaders, found by their id under the registered resource prefixes; figure textures are painted with QPainter.
