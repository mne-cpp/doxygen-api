Detailed Description

The chain of transforms from MEG head coordinates to MNI and FreeSurfer Talairach coordinates.

Head -> surface RAS (the FreeSurfer MRI frame) comes from the coregistration, surface RAS -> scanner RAS from the centre (c_ras) of the subject's MRI, scanner RAS -> MNI Talairach from mri/transforms/talairach.xfm, and MNI -> FreeSurfer Talairach from the fixed Brett approximations MNE-C uses (one matrix above and one below the AC-PC plane). Empty transforms mark links that are not known.
