mne::MNENamedMatrix is the C++ port of fiff_named_matrix: an Eigen dense matrix carrying explicit StringList row and column names. Leadfields, SSP vectors, CTF compensators and many derived matrices are stored in this form so callers can re-order or subset by channel name without depending on positional indices.
