One input stream "eeg" in the SDK's format: an int32 per channel, with each channel's gain, offset and exponent as its scale and offset (volts for the analog inputs). The ExG inputs are EEG 001..EEG nnn, the digital inputs STI 014. A change of the digital word away from its value at the start is pushed as an event at that sample, with the word as code.
