Detailed Description

ChannelDataModel is the Qt face of mne::scene::TraceBuffer, which stores the samples of the most recent N seconds per channel and resolves the channels' scales, colours and bad flags. The model describes the channels of a FiffInfo to it (names, types, per-kind scales and colours), keeps the FiffInfo's bad list in step and turns the buffer's change notifications into Qt signals.
