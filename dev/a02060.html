Detailed Description

Widgets-free core of the 3-D brain view: scene wiring, camera, multi-view, picking, overlay and frame rendering.

BrainViewCore holds everything of the interactive 3-D brain scene that does not need Qt Widgets. It connects mne::disp::BrainTreeModel data changes to scene rebuilds, translates QtGui mouse / wheel / key events for the toolkit-neutral mne::scene::BrainViewInteraction (orbit, pan, zoom, pane selection and splitters), takes the camera matrices from mne::scene::OrbitCamera, paints the 2-D overlay (FPS, per-pane info, pane names, hovered region, pane separators) of mne::scene::BrainViewOverlay with paintScene() into a QImage and records a frame through mne::disp::BrainRenderer into a QRhi render target.

A host surface implements mne::disp::BrainViewHost and forwards its events and frames: mne::disp::BrainView (a QRhiWidget) and mne::disp::BrainWindow (a QWindow owning its own QRhi).
