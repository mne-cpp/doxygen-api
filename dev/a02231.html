ProjectorsView builds a QCheckBox per FiffProj loaded from the active dataset, plus an "all active / inactive" master toggle, and emits projSelectionChanged whenever the user flips one. The rtprocessing pipeline uses the resulting active mask to (de)project the streaming data on the fly.
