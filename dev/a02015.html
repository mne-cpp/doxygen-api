The implementation serialises the project metadata (name, description, mna_version, creation/modification timestamps) followed by the MnaSubject list and the MnaNode pipeline. The mna_version field is written from MnaProject::CURRENT_SCHEMA_VERSION so older readers can refuse projects from a future schema, while extras carries forward-compatible additions through the round-trip without loss.
