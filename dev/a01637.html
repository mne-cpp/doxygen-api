BrainFlow's file streamer (add_streamer("file://<path>:w")) and DataFilter.write_file write a sample per line, the board's rows separated by tabs. The target is the file, board= the board that recorded it, whose layout gives the channels of brainflow://. The replay is paced in real time or as fast as it is read, once or in a loop; markers become events and the timestamp row is the clock.
