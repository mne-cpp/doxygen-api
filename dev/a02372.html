Detailed Description

Triangle descriptor with cached centroid, area and normal vectors.

MNELIB::MNETriangle is the per-face record produced by MNELIB::MNESourceSpace and MNELIB::MNEBemSurface readers so downstream code (forward solver, projection-to-surface, rendering) does not have to recompute these quantities. Carries the three vertex indices, the centroid, the un-normalised and normalised triangle normals and the area.
