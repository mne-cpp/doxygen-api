The one Qt builder of the toolkit-neutral settings description: check boxes, spin boxes, sliders, combo boxes, line edits, file choosers and buttons in group boxes, kept in step with the form both ways, with the enabled and visible conditions of the fields applied. Values persist in QSettings.
