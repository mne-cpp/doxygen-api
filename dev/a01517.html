RtConnectivity estimates the connectivity networks of mne::compute_connectivity (coherence, imaginary coherence, PLI, wPLI, dwPLI, USPLI, PLV, correlation, ...) over the newest trials of a stream. It keeps the tapered spectra and cross-spectra of every trial in the window together with their sums, so a new trial costs its own spectra plus the final normalization, and the oldest trial is subtracted from the sums instead of recomputing the rest. It is synchronous: call it from whichever thread receives the trials.
