AverageSceneItem is a fixed-size mini-plot drawn for every channel by mne::disp::AverageScene. It stores the per-condition data vectors, their QColor mapping and the y-scale settings, then paints the overlaid traces, the t = 0 marker and the baseline inside its bounding rect during paint().
