RtInvOp maps streaming MEG / EEG data to source estimates with the MNE, dSPM or sLORETA inverse of mne::MNEInverseOperator and mne::InvMinimumNorm, and follows each new noise covariance, e.g. from mne::RtCov. Everything that depends only on the forward solution (channel selection, depth and orientation priors, the source-weighted gain covariance G·R·Gᵀ) is computed once per channel set. A new covariance then costs the whitener and one eigendecomposition of a channels × channels matrix instead of the SVD of the whitened gain, plus, for dSPM and sLORETA, one channels × channels by channels × sources product for the noise normalization. The kernel is applied in factored form, so it is never assembled. It is synchronous: call it from whichever thread suits the application.
