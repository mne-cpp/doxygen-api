Detailed Description

Describes the frames of the brain view as GpuFrame for any backend.

A frame is begun with begin(), filled pane by pane with surfaces, instanced glyphs and the screen overlay in drawing order, and taken with finish(). Meshes are identified by a key of the caller; their streams are copied into resources() when their generation changes, and dropped when a frame does not draw them for a while or on release().

Since
3.0.0
