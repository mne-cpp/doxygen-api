Detailed Description

Toolkit-free model of a tree view: its nodes, which of them are expanded, and the selected node.

Panels hold item trees and hosts show them, e.g. viz::qt::ItemTreeView in Qt and FormView without a toolkit. Nodes are addressed by ids that stay valid until the node is removed; owners may choose the ids, e.g. those of their own model, with set_items(). The rows a host shows are the visible nodes: the roots and the children of expanded nodes, depth first. One node at a time is selected. Up and Down move the selection between the visible nodes, Right expands a node or goes to its first child and Left collapses it or goes to its parent. Delete asks the remove handler, if any, to remove the selected node; the tree itself does not remove it.
