Control3DView wraps a QTreeView whose model is the same Data3DTreeModel that drives the 3-D scene, so toggling / editing items here (BEM surfaces, source spaces, sensor positions, MRI volumes, source estimates …) propagates directly to the renderer. A custom QStyledItemDelegate paints colour swatches, sliders and combo boxes inline.
