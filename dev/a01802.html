Replaces LayoutScene and the QGraphicsView it drove: the wheel and pinch zoom around the pointer, the right mouse button and pan gestures move the view, a double click fits the contents, and a left drag outside an item spans a rubber band. Until the user zooms or pans, the view keeps its contents fitted as it resizes.
