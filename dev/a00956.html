ModalitySelectionView builds one QCheckBox per channel-modality present in the active FiffInfo and emits a modalitiesChanged list whenever the user toggles one. It is consumed primarily by mne::disp::ButterflyView and mne::disp::AverageLayoutView to hide entire modalities at once.
