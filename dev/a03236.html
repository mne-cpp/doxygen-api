Detailed Description

RtRawDisplay prepares streamed raw data for display: SSP, compensator, SPHARA, filter and triggers.

A real-time raw viewer corrects every incoming block with the active SSP projectors and the CTF compensator, detects trigger flanks on one stimulus channel, optionally reduces noise with SPHARA and filters a selection of channels with FIR kernels. RtRawDisplay does all of that block by block and keeps the filter overlap between blocks; the caller places the results into its display buffer. It has no GUI dependency; the Qt model of the raw viewer (viz::qt::RtFiffRawViewModel) is an adapter around it.
