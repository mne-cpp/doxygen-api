LinePlot accepts one or several std::vector<double> y-series (with an optional matching x-series) and renders them as connected polylines with auto-scaled axes, a title and x/y labels. It is intentionally lightweight (no Qt Charts / OpenGL dependency) and is the fallback plotting primitive used by HPI / coregistration dialogs and by diagnostic test apps that just need a small embedded line chart.
