Detailed Description

Container item that groups raw FIFF digitizer points by category (Cardinal, HPI, EEG, Extra).

Takes the flat digitizer points of the FIFF info block and fans it into one mne::disp::DigitizerTreeItem child per category with the canonical colour scheme (Nasion green, LPA red, RPA blue, HPI dark-red, EEG cyan, Extra magenta) and per-category sphere radius (2 mm fiducials, 1 mm everything else).

Children are rendered as a single batched-sphere mesh per category so a 5000-point head shape is still one draw call.
