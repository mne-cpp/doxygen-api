ChannelWindow creates its own QRhi, swap chain and render pass descriptor, renders a mne::disp::ChannelRhiRenderer frame on every expose and update request and forwards its input events to the renderer. It only needs QtGui. A Qt Widgets application embeds it with createWindowContainer(channelWindow, parent).
