mne::MNESurface is the minimal geometric surface object that the cortex-based algorithms in mne_mne and mne_inv share: it carries no decimation or patch information and is used wherever only the topology is needed (projection to surface, distance maps, head-shape fitting). Heavier flavours - mne::MNESourceSpace and mne::MNEBemSurface - subclass or wrap it.
