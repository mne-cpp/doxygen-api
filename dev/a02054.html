ButterflyView is a QWidget that paints all averaged traces of the active mne::viz::qt::EvokedSetModel with QPainter, decimated to the widget width. Per-modality visibility (MAG / GRAD / EEG) is toggled through mne::viz::qt::ModalitySelectionView and the colour / selection of conditions through mne::viz::qt::AverageSelectionView. The widget is the workhorse evoked viewer in MNE-Scan and is intended to live as a tab inside a mne::viz::qt::MultiView dock.
