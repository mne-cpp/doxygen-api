Detailed Description

SettingsForm describes a settings panel (fields, ranges, choices, groups) and holds its values.

A panel is described once without a GUI toolkit: its fields with their kind, label, range, unit and choices, in groups, and when a field is enabled or shown. The form holds the values, keeps them valid and tells listeners about changes. A toolkit needs one generic builder that turns any form into widgets (viz::qt::SettingsPanel for Qt Widgets) instead of one hand-written view per panel.
