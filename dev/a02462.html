Detailed Description

Camera state and projection / view / model matrix computation for single- and multi-view layouts.

CameraController is the Qt-typed adapter of the toolkit-neutral mne::viz::OrbitCamera, which does the maths. It is widget-agnostic: it takes the visible scene centroid + extent and a per-pane mne::viz::qt::SubView and produces the mne::viz::qt::CameraResult bundle (projection, view, model, eye position, look-at and up vector) consumed by mne::viz::qt::BrainRenderer.

It also encodes the mouse-interaction conventions used everywhere in mne_viz_qt: left-drag rotates via quaternion accumulation, wheel zooms by exponential scaling around the scene centre, middle-drag pans by projecting screen-space deltas into the camera's local right / up basis, and the multi-view perspective pane orbits while the orthographic Top / Left / Front panes only pan + zoom.
