MNELIB::MNEHemisphere is the visualisation-friendly counterpart of MNESourceSpace: in addition to vertices, triangles and the MNE decimation it carries pre-computed vertex normals, triangle centroids, tri-areas, the patch information used by surface-oriented inverse solvers and a colour table sourced from a FreeSurfer annotation. It is the unit DISP3D draws from and that morphing (MNELIB::MNEMorphMap), labelling and cluster operations operate on.
