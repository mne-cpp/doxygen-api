Detailed Description

Executes a GpuFrame on the CPU.

Triangles are rasterized with perspective-correct varyings, near- and far-plane clipping, depth testing and face culling; lines and points are one pixel wide, as on the GPU backends. Figure textures are rasterized with to_raster(). The shaders of the views in mne_scene are registered on construction; set_shader() adds others. Draws whose shader is unknown are skipped.
