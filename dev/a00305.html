Detailed Description

Zero-copy views into a stream ring: AcqBlockView (read) and AcqWriteView (write).

A view addresses committed (read) or reserved (write) samples in place: one contiguous part, or two when the block wraps around the end of a ring that is not mirror-mapped. Views own nothing, do not allocate and are valid until released (AcqReader::release) or committed (AcqWriter::commit).

Element (channel c, sample s) of a part is at data + c * channelStride + s * sampleStride, so interleaved and planar rings, and any channel subset, are handled by the same code.
