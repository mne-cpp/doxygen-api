Detailed Description

Butterfly viewer overlaying every channel of one or more averaged conditions.

Paints all visible traces with QPainter; visibility per modality and per condition is driven by ModalitySelectionView and AverageSelectionView. Used as the main evoked viewer in MNE-Scan.
