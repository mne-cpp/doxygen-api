Lossless conversion between AcqStreamInfo and mne::FiffInfo.

Part of the separate bridge library mne_acq_fiff, the only mne_acq component that depends on FIFF (and therefore Qt); mne_acq itself stays standard-library only.

Round trip: per-channel fields map to AcqChannelInfo (name, kind, unit, scale = cal * range) and are kept verbatim in AcqChannelInfo::fiff; everything else of FiffInfo is stored serialized in an AcqExtension with schema "fiff.MeasInfo/1". toFiffInfo(fromFiffInfo(x)) equals x field by field. When a channel was edited in AcqStreamInfo, the edited per-channel fields win.
