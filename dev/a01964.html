One input stream "raw" in the buffer's sample format, from the samples put after start(). With the Neuromag header chunk (neuromag2ft), the channels and the measurement info, including the digitizer points of the isotrak chunk, are those of the acquisition, its floats physical values, and changes of STI 014 (or STI101) events. Otherwise the channels are named after the channel-name chunk, or numbered. Events put in the buffer with a numeric value become events with that code. A new header (a new measurement) ends the stream.
