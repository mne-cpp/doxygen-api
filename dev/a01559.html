One input stream "eeg" in the SDK's format: a double per channel, in volts for the inputs. The referential inputs are EEG 001..EEG nnn, the bipolar ones BIP 1..BIP n, the trigger word STI 014 and the sample counter COUNTER. A change of the trigger word away from its value at the start is pushed as an event at that sample.
