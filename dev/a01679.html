streaming_board:// : the samples a BrainFlow session streams with add_streamer, without BrainFlow.

BrainFlow's streamer ("streaming_board://<address>:<port>") sends a board's samples as UDP datagrams of whole samples, num_rows doubles each; BrainFlow's STREAMING_BOARD reads them. This backend reads them too: the target is the address and port, board= the board behind the stream, whose layout comes from a table generated from BrainFlow or, for other boards, from BrainFlow when it is installed. The stream is that of brainflow://, and the sender's marker row becomes events. simulate=1 streams a synthetic:// session in the board's layout to itself.
