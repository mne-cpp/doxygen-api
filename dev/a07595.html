Detailed Description

Head-up display of the 3-D brain view: FPS, view info, hovered region, pane names and separators.

The overlay keeps the text and geometry of its items in logical pixels; update_layout() places them for the current view size and pane layout, and draw() renders the visible ones (separators below labels) as a display list that a backend paints over the 3-D scene.

3.0.0
