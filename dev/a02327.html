mne::MNEMshDisplaySurface attaches an mne::MNESurface to a shading state - per-vertex colours, scalar overlays, current mne::MNEMshColorScaleDef and visibility flags - producing the self-contained record that the MSH renderer (and its mne-cpp DISP3D successor) draws.
