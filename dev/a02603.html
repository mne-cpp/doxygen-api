mne::FiffDigPointSet holds the head-coordinate point cloud associated with one recording: cardinal fiducials, HPI coil positions, EEG electrodes and the extra head-shape samples. It is what mne::FiffStream returns when asked for the contents of an FIFFB_ISOTRAK / FIFFB_HPI_MEAS block, and what mne::FiffDigitizerData consumes when constructing a digitization view for the registration GUIs. Round-trips with the info['dig'] list in MNE-Python.
