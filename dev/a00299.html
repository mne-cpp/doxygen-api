Signal-Space Projection (SSP) removes a low-rank subspace from MEG/EEG data; the projector itself is stored under FIFFB_PROJ / FIFFB_PROJ_ITEM tags. FIFFLIB::FiffProj is the C++ wrapper for one such item: a description, the kind (FIFFV_PROJ_ITEM_FIELD, FIFFV_PROJ_ITEM_EEG_AVREF, ...), the active flag, the desired flag and the named matrix carrying the projection vectors keyed by channel name. The list of FIFFLIB::FiffProj inside FiffInfo::projs is what FIFFLIB::FiffRawData and FIFFLIB::FiffEvoked apply (or de-apply) via make_projector during raw / evoked processing, with field-for-field parity to mne.Projection / mne.compute_proj_* in MNE-Python.
