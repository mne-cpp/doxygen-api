MNELIB::MNEInverseOperator is the C++ port of -inv.fif: it stores the noise covariance, the source covariance, the orientation priors and the left/right singular vectors needed to assemble the Tikhonov-regularised inverse for a given SNR and method (MNE, dSPM, sLORETA). It composes the forward solution (MNELIB::MNEForwardSolution), covariance (FIFFLIB::FiffCov) and source space (MNELIB::MNESourceSpaces) into a single object that MNELIB::MinimumNorm in INVERSELIB consumes to produce MNESourceEstimate time courses.
