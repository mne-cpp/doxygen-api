Implements FIFF read/write of the gain matrix, source-space attachment, coordinate-frame conversion, label-restricted clustering using mne::KMeans, gain-matrix whitening against an mne::FiffCov noise covariance and SSP/CTF application. Mirrors mne.forward of MNE-Python and the mne_inverse_operator C tool.
