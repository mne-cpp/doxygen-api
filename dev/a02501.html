Detailed Description

Wire format of the Lab Streaming Layer as liblsl 1.10+ speaks it (internal to mne_lsl).

Discovery: a resolver sends "LSL:shortinfo\r\n<predicate>\r\n<return port> <query id>\r\n" by UDP to port 16571 (multicast, broadcast and localhost); every matching outlet answers "<query id>\r\n<shortinfo XML>" to the return port. Time correction: "LSL:timedata\r\n<wave id> <t0>\r\n" to the outlet's service port is answered with " <wave id> <t0> <t1> <t2>". Data: a TCP client sends "LSL:streamfeed/110 <uid>\r\n" and headers, the outlet answers "LSL/110 200 OK" and headers, then two test-pattern samples, then the samples. A sample is a tag byte (1: timestamp deduced from the previous one and the rate, 2: an 8-byte timestamp follows) and the channel values in the outlet's byte order; strings carry a length-size byte, the length and the bytes.
