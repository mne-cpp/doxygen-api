The functions in this header scan rows of a data matrix — typically the stimulus / digital-trigger channels of a MEG or EEG recording — for samples that reach a threshold, either in value (detect_trigger_flanks_max) or in their sample-to-sample change (detect_trigger_flanks_grad), and return the sample index and value of every detected flank. After a flank, a burst length of samples is skipped so that one pulse is reported once.

to_event_matrix turns the detected flanks of each channel into the three-column event layout (sample, previous value, new value) used by the rest of mne-cpp.
