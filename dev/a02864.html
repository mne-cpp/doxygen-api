FormView lays out, draws and edits any SettingsForm without a toolkit.

FormView is a SurfaceClient, so every backend that hosts a surface (Qt, SDL, ...) shows any Panel through it without a view written for the panel. It draws with a Canvas in the look of a Theme and edits the form with the pointer, the wheel and the keyboard.
