Detailed Description

FontFace reads one face of a TrueType font for the software text renderer.

The reader covers what drawing Latin, Greek and Cyrillic text needs: metrics, the character map, glyph outlines (simple and composite) and pair kerning from GPOS or the kern table. Hinting, shaping and CFF outlines are out of scope. Every read is bounds-checked, so damaged files fail to load or yield empty glyphs instead of crashing.
