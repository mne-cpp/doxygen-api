mne::MNEMghTagGroup gathers every tag present at the tail of an MGH volume and offers typed accessors for the commonly used kinds (MGH_TAG_OLD_COLORTABLE, MGH_TAG_MGH_XFORM, MGH_TAG_CMDLINE, ...) so callers do not have to walk the raw byte stream.
