mne::MNESssData captures the inside/outside basis orders (LIn, LOut), the device-to-head transformation in effect at the time of Maxwell filtering and the multipole moments computed by MaxFilter. The information lives in FIFFB_SSS_INFO / FIFFB_SSS_CAL_ADJUST blocks and is required to keep downstream forward / inverse computations consistent with the SSS-filtered data.
