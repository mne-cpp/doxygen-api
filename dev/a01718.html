AveragingSettingsView exposes the parameters needed to compute event related averages from a continuous FIFF stream: stimulus channel, trigger types of interest, pre- and post-stimulus window in milliseconds, baseline-correction interval and an embedded artefact threshold table backed by DISPLIB::ArtifactSettingsView. Edits propagate as Qt signals that the rtprocessing averaging job consumes.
