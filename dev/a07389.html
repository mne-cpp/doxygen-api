Detailed Description

The vertices of all traces over a window around the view, and what to draw of them.

stream() fetches the vertices of every logical channel for the visible samples plus a prefetch of prefetch_factor() view widths on each side, decimated to the view's scale; scrolling needs no new vertices until the view uses up half of the prefetch on a side where the window does not end with the data. draws() lists the traces of the current frame with their shader parameters.

Since
3.0.0

Lists the traces of a frame; the uniform slot of a trace is its position in the list.

In butterfly mode every logical channel is drawn in the lane of its type, otherwise the visible rows top to bottom. Bad channels are red, hidden or missing ones are flat and of the background colour.
