Detailed Description

Single off-grid focal dipole result with free position, moment and fit-quality metrics.

mne::InvFocalDipole captures one equivalent current dipole that is not constrained to a source-space vertex — typically the output of the ECD dipole-fit pipeline or of a max-power beamformer. Position is in head-coordinate metres, moment is in Am, and the goodness, chi-squared and degrees-of-freedom fields propagate fit diagnostics to downstream viewers and report writers. A list of mne::InvFocalDipole instances is attached to mne::InvSourceEstimate to carry sparse focal results alongside dense grid estimates.
