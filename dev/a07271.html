Detailed Description

An interactive figure in scene coordinates: its bounds, how it draws and how it answers the pointer.

A host (FigureView without a toolkit, mne::viz::qt::SceneViewport in Qt) maps the scene to its window and passes clicks, hovers and rubber bands in scene coordinates. A zoomable figure is fitted, zoomed and panned by the host; a figure that is not zoomable lays itself out to the view size given in resize() and draws in view pixels, the host only scrolling it vertically. The figure tells its hosts to repaint through changed().
