Detailed Description

Splits a view into 1 to 4 panes with draggable splitters.

Two panes sit side by side, three panes are one full-width pane above two, four panes form a 2 x 2 grid. The splitters sit at fractions of the width and height, clamped to 0.15 .. 0.85, and no pane gets narrower than the minimum pane size.

Since
3.0.0
