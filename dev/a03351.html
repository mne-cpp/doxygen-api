Read MEG and EEG channel information from a FIFF file, excluding bad channels.

Opens the file, reads measurement info, and splits channels into MEG and EEG lists (in that order), skipping channels in the bads list. EEG channels must pass FiffChInfo::isValidEeg().

Parameters
[in]namePath to the FIFF measurement file.
[in]do_megIf true, include MEG channels.
[in]do_eegIf true, include EEG channels.
[in]badsList of bad channel names to exclude.
[out]chspCombined list of accepted channels (MEG first, then EEG).
[out]nmegpNumber of MEG channels in chsp.
[out]neegpNumber of EEG channels in chsp.
Returns
true on success, false on error.
