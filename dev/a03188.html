RtAveraging cuts an epoch around every onset on a stim channel of the incoming blocks and keeps the mean of the newest epochs per event code, so the evoked responses follow the current state of the subject. An epoch is averaged as soon as its last sample arrives; epochs may overlap and reach back into earlier blocks.
