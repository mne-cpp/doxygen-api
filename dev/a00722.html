Every functional-connectivity estimator in mne_connectivity produces one mne::NetworkEdge per ordered channel pair (or per unordered pair for symmetric metrics) and adds it to the mne::Network. The edge owns the full weight matrix returned by the metric - one row per frequency bin - and an inclusive [first, last] frequency-bin window that reduces the matrix to the single scalar weight used by thresholding and display.
