TFplot accepts a spectrogram as produced by mne::Spectrogram::make_spectrogram (rows = the first N/2 frequency bins of an N-point FFT, columns = the N time samples) together with the sample rate, an optional frequency band, a colour map and the time of the first sample, and draws it with ImageSc: time in seconds, frequency in Hz and power normalized to its maximum in the band.
