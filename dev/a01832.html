The data acquisition of a Neuromag Vectorview or TRIUX system hands every client the FIFF tags of the running measurement: at its start the measurement info (FIFFB_MEAS_INFO), then FIFF_DATA_BUFFER tags of 32-bit integers, one per channel and sample, whose physical value is raw * cal * range.
