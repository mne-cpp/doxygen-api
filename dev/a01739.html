ScalingView assembles a mne::viz::qt::ScaleControl per channel modality in the active FiffInfo (MAG in fT, GRAD in fT/cm, EEG in µV, …) and emits a scalingChanged map every time the user nudges one. Consumed by mne::viz::qt::RtFiffRawView, mne::viz::qt::ChannelDataView and mne::viz::qt::ButterflyView to translate raw SI units into screen units.
