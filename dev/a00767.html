mne::InvEcd represents one ECD record produced by the dipole- fit pipeline at one time point: the head-frame position, the dipole moment, the goodness-of-fit, the χ² value and the degrees of freedom / function-evaluation count used by the optimiser. The class mirrors the ecdRec record of MNE-C and is the atomic element collected into the mne::InvEcdSet returned by mne::InvDipoleFit.
