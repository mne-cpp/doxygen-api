Detailed Description

The magnitude response of a filter kernel in dB from 0 Hz to the Nyquist frequency, with its cut-off frequencies marked, as a SceneFigure.

The figure is not zoomable: it lays the FilterResponsePlot out to the view size. picture() renders it at any size, e.g. to export it with write_svg() or to_raster().
