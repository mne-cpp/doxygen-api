Detailed Description

Combined MEG/EEG measurement data with inverse operator, field mapping, dipole fitting, and display state.

This is the central "workspace" structure that ties together raw/averaged measurement data, the inverse operator, field-mapping engines, dipole-fit state, digitiser points, display scales, and current viewer state.
