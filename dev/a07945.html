ColorMap exposes pure-function valueToXxx(double v) accessors that map a value in \([0,1]\) to a QRgb; the maps themselves are Qt-free in mne::viz (colormap.h). Bundled palettes include the perceptually-uniform Viridis, the MATLAB classics (Jet, Hot, Bone, Cool), a diverging Red-Blue map for signed data and the asymmetric MNE-Python hot/ hotNeg variants used by the source-estimate overlays.
