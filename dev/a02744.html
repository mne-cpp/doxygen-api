Detailed Description

Preferences for MEG/EEG field mapping including interpolation grades, smoothing, and sphere model origins.

Controls the icosahedron subdivision level, smoothing strength, integration radius, and sphere-model origins (MEG and EEG) that drive the field-interpolation pipeline.
