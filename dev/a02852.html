Detailed Description

CBOR (RFC 8949) values, arrays and maps in standard C++, the counterpart of Qt's QCborValue, QCborArray and QCborMap.

CborValue, CborArray and CborMap follow their Qt counterparts call for call, so porting is mostly a rename. The differences are:

  • Text is UTF-8 in std::string, byte strings are std::vector<std::byte>.
  • Map keys are text. Decoding turns integer keys into their decimal text, as QCborMap::toJsonObject() does, and rejects other key types.
  • Simple values other than false, true, null and undefined are rejected when decoding.

Encoding uses definite lengths and the shortest integer heads, and writes doubles as 64-bit floats, which gives the same bytes as QCborValue::toCbor(). Decoding also accepts indefinite lengths and 16- and 32-bit floats. Nothing throws on malformed input: decoding reports a CborParseError.
