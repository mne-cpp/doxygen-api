RtFiffRawViewDelegate transforms each visible row of the mne::viz::qt::RtFiffRawViewModel into a polyline drawn inside the cell rectangle. It applies the active y-scaling, the SSP / compensation operators, the trigger overlays and the bad-channel greying, and is shared by mne::viz::qt::ChannelDataView and the rolling raw browser.
